# First Ten Progression Pilot Protocol v1

## Purpose and boundary

Evaluate the actual A1 sequence positions 1–10 across multiple days. The pilot tests
delivery viability and progression hypotheses; it does not establish general efficacy,
CEFR certification, production reliability, or the final lesson count.

No collection begins until all ten rendered videos are transcript-matched and
listened-to, the continuous prototype passes, and facilitator rehearsal is complete.

## Participants

Recruit eight Arabic-dominant adults at a genuine beginner boundary. Six eligible
complete journeys are the minimum for a progression decision. Exclude course authors,
the owner, people previously exposed to answer-bearing materials, minors, and learners
who already demonstrate the first-ten exit tasks independently at baseline.

Recruit up to ten people to allow honest withdrawal; never pressure a participant to
complete merely to satisfy the minimum.

## Schedule

Use six visits across 8–12 days, maximum 60 minutes each:

| Visit | Content | Required observation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | U1-L01, U1-L02 | first-contact usability and listening baseline |
| 2 | U1-L03, U1-L04 | input-to-output progression and information-gap repair |
| 3 | U1-L05, U1-L06 | dense listening load followed by lower-load reading |
| 4 | U1-L07 | delayed probes plus form transfer and digital instructions |
| 5 | U1-L08, U1-L09 | profile load and transfer into written interaction |
| 6 | Unit 1 mastery, U2-L01 | integration, unit transition, and delayed retention |

Record absence, pause, stop, reschedule, and withdrawal. Do not collapse ten lessons
into one sitting or silently omit a difficult lesson.

## Per-lesson flow

1. Record version and entry state; do not pre-teach.
2. Show the one-teacher explanation video.
3. Run the authored post-video activities.
4. Run coached practice; count answer-revealing and non-answer-revealing help.
5. Run the fresh mastery task with field or situation support only.
6. If help reveals an answer, use the one equivalent replacement task; maximum two
   fresh attempts.
7. Record four rubric dimensions, time, corrections, fatigue, difficulty, boredom,
   value, and controlled issue codes.
8. Schedule the named delayed probe without showing the prior answer.

Completion, video viewing, recognition choices, coached success, and teach-back cannot
override a failed fresh performance.

## Unit and transition evidence

After U1-L09, administer all three fresh Unit 1 stations. Then present U2-L01 without
re-teaching its recycled family language. Record whether Unit 1 writing and family
language support the new listening task or whether the transition exposes a gap.

## Load and split hypotheses

U1-L08 is the primary split candidate; U1-L05 is secondary. A split review is mandatory
if any named lesson meets two or more of these conditions among eligible completers:

- median active time exceeds its upper hypothesis by more than 10%;
- 40% or more report fatigue 4–5 out of 5;
- 50% or more fail two distinct required constructs on both fresh attempts;
- 35% or more need answer-revealing help before an assessable attempt; or
- delayed performance is at least 20 percentage points below the unit median.

This review may split, reorder, narrow, or redistribute targets. The count of 45 A1
capabilities is not protected from observed evidence.

## Decision precedence

1. `stop_and_repair`: any consent, privacy, safety, answer-leakage, or unusable-media
   gate fails.
2. `insufficient_evidence`: fewer than six eligible complete journeys or a required
   metric lacks its denominator.
3. `revise_and_repilot`: a hard learning/usability gate or split trigger fails while
   safety remains intact.
4. `go_to_system_revision`: all hard gates pass; monitoring risks have named owners.

Publish only the de-identified aggregate, lesson revisions, media/version decisions,
and final decision. Private longitudinal records never enter the repository.
