# Facilitator Checklist

## Before the first visit

- Confirm adult, Arabic-dominant beginner eligibility without collecting identity.
- Read the Arabic consent text and record explicit voluntary consent.
- Assign a random study code; keep any scheduling contact outside study records.
- Confirm the exact package and rendered-media versions.
- Explain that fictional information is always acceptable.

## Every visit

- Reconfirm consent and willingness, then ask about fatigue or accessibility needs.
- Do not show a transcript before a listening capture.
- Do not answer an applied question during the explanation video.
- Let the learner attempt before helping; code the help type and count.
- After answer-revealing help, use the equivalent replacement mastery task.
- Never score coached practice as mastery.
- Stop at 60 minutes or earlier if the learner requests it.
- Record a break, stop, return, or withdrawal honestly.

## Double rating

- Live double-rate at least two full participant journeys.
- Raters score independently before discussing differences.
- Never preserve raw learner language to settle disagreement; use the controlled rubric
  and issue codes.

## After each visit

- Validate the private JSON record.
- Keep it outside the repository with restricted access.
- Do not record or copy names, answers, free-text notes, audio, video, or screenshots into it.
- Record media or interface defects against lesson and version, not participant identity.
