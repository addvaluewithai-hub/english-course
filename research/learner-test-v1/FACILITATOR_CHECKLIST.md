# Facilitator Checklist

## Before the participant arrives

- Open the local prototype and confirm `U1-L03` appears.
- Confirm the final transcript link opens and no network request is required.
- Choose the condition from the allocation rule: `teachback` or `omit`.
- Open `observer.html` separately and assign the next de-identified session ID.
- Keep the gold assessment rubric available; do not keep a full answer model visible.
- If this is one of the double-rated sessions, seat the second evaluator silently.
- Do not start raw audio, video, or screen recording.

## Consent and setup

- Read or show `CONSENT_AR.md` and obtain all four confirmations.
- Confirm the learner is an Arabic-speaking adult and record only the entry band.
- Encourage fictional personal details.
- Ask whether an accessibility support is wanted; never request a diagnosis.
- Explain that the lesson is being tested, not the learner.

## During the lesson

- Let the learner navigate; help only after an observable pause or direct request.
- Do not interrupt the explanation video with applied questions.
- Record section duration and issue codes, not raw learner wording.
- During coached practice, permit the configured correction and retry.
- Before fresh mastery, hide the model and sentence frames.
- Ask for the final response aloud. Text input may support the prototype but cannot by
  itself prove the speaking outcome.
- If answer-revealing help is given, reclassify the attempt as practice and use one
  equivalent fresh variant.
- Keep teach-back after mastery and score it separately.

## Stop and safety rules

- If the learner asks to stop, record `stopped_incomplete` and close respectfully.
- If immediate danger, self-harm, or abuse appears, pause the lesson, encourage contact
  with local emergency help or a trusted person, record only `safety_paused`, and do
  not make a mastery judgment.
- Never record the crisis details in the research note.

## After the lesson

- Obtain the short experience ratings without coaching the answers.
- Complete the four rubric dimensions from the fresh spoken performance only.
- Mark completion and mastery separately.
- Keep issue notes de-identified and under 240 characters.
- Download the observer record, validate it, and store it in the restricted pilot
  location—not in this public repository.
- Tell the participant their session ID and the 90-day withdrawal route.
