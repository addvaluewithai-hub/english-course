# Gold Lesson Asset Briefs

## Visuals

1. **First-meeting hero:** two diverse adults at a community or study event, warm and
   calm, no national stereotypes, enough empty space for Arabic and English text.
2. **Five field icons:** name badge, origin globe, home pin, work bag, interest spark.
3. **Origin versus residence card:** one person, country marker, and current-city home;
   the contrast must remain understandable without colour.

AI illustrations are contextual, not evidence of real people or places. Do not place
generated text inside images.

## Audio

- One natural adult model at a slow but connected A1 pace.
- Separate clips for each model line and each pronunciation contrast.
- A second voice for the fresh prompt; do not reuse the model speaker.
- Preserve natural contractions. Avoid exaggerated syllable-by-syllable speech.

## Video hypothesis

Optional 35-50 second version of the model plus the origin/residence contrast. Captions
and the complete readable route are mandatory. The prototype may use text and browser
speech; video is not required to test the learning sequence.

## Accessibility

Every decorative image has empty alt text; instructional visuals have concise Arabic
and English alternatives. Captions, transcript, pause, replay, and reduced-motion
behavior are required before learner testing.
