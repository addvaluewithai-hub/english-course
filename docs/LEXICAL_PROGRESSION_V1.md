# Lexical Progression v1

Status: accepted internal curriculum contract.

Oxford provides 5,322 levelled entry lines as a reference pool, not 5,322 mandatory targets.
Every selected item must serve an approved communicative owner and pass the lifecycle gates.

| Resource family | First guided use | First family consolidation | A1 | C2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core high-frequency headwords and meanings | A1 | A1 | Control a small outcome-bound set of concrete words and meanings for immediate personal situations. | Choose precise lexical senses for subtle distinctions without treating rarity as proficiency. |
| Formulaic chunks and interactional frames | A1 | A1 | Use short learnt chunks for greeting, identity, immediate needs, thanks, and basic repair. | Reshape conventional and creative phraseology precisely for stance, nuance, and audience. |
| Topic and domain lexis | A1 | A1 | Control bounded words for personal details, family, time, home, places, food, services, and safety. | Build task-specific expert repertoires through documented corpus and domain evidence. |
| Collocation and lexical partnership | A2 | B2 | Notice and reproduce whole high-value combinations inside learnt chunks. | Exploit conventional and marked combinations deliberately for precision, tone, and rhetorical effect. |
| Word families and productive word formation | A2 | B1 | Notice transparent members only when they serve a learnt concrete outcome. | Use morphological choices precisely for compression, stance, register, and stylistic effect. |
| Phrasal verbs and other multiword lexical items | A2 | B1 | Recognise only indispensable whole multiword items inside immediate-use chunks. | Handle subtle, idiomatic, and stylistically marked multiword choices with precision. |
| Multiple meanings and sense selection | A2 | B1 | Learn one outcome-relevant meaning at a time and avoid implying full headword mastery. | Control fine sense boundaries, ambiguity, and deliberate double meaning where appropriate. |
| Register and situational appropriateness | B1 | B2 | Recognise a basic polite choice versus a neutral direct choice in learnt situations. | Control subtle register mixtures, markedness, voice, and interpersonal effect. |
| Idiomatic and colloquial repertoire | B2 | C1 | Recognise only essential fixed social formulas; idiomatic range is not an exit demand. | Use very broad idiomatic and colloquial language with precise social and stylistic judgement. |
| Connotation, semantic precision, and rhetorical choice | C1 | C2 | Distinguish only meaning contrasts necessary for immediate concrete tasks. | Exploit fine semantic, connotative, affective, and rhetorical distinctions consistently. |

## C2 boundary

There is no invented C2 list or word-count target. A C2 item needs an approved outcome,
registered contemporary usage evidence, domain evidence when specialist, and explicit
sense, collocation, register, connotation, control, and recycling decisions.

Exact A1 headwords, meanings, chunks, and recycle locations are selected in SPINE-005.
