# First Ten Lessons: Content QA v1

Status: `pass_for_continuous_prototype_pending_external_learner_evidence`.

This is a structured primary review, not external learner evidence or an
independent second-team review. It approves source packages for one continuous
prototype; it does not approve rendered media or course release.

## Global result

- 10 sequence-authentic lesson packages;
- 120 required package files;
- 65 exact item roles;
- no common-contract or gold-QA error;
- one-teacher videos with post-video application; and
- fresh primary and equivalent replacement mastery tasks.

## Lesson review

| Lesson | Load decision | Review judgment | Monitoring hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1-L01 | standard | Opening, response congruence, and closing form one coherent social task. | Check whether three pronunciation foci distract true beginners from the four social moves. |
| U1-L02 | standard | Two-pass listening and referent tracking precede productive self-introduction appropriately. | Confirm that bounded I/he/she exposure is clear without implying full productive pronoun control. |
| U1-L03 | standard | Owner-accepted gold lesson remains intact at its final third position and passes separate gold QA. | Compare its pacing and perceived quality with neighbouring lessons rather than treating it as an isolated benchmark. |
| U1-L04 | elevated_monitor | Question purpose and communication repair belong in one authentic information-gap capability. | Eight introductions may overload learners if spelling and prominence compete with question-answer meaning. |
| U1-L05 | elevated_monitor | Name, number, and day capture creates one coherent accuracy task with meaning-relevant sound support. | Number range, days, grouping, and four sound foci create the densest listening lesson in the unit. |
| U1-L06 | light_integration | Field-directed reading provides a deliberate lower-load bridge after dense listening. | Check that prose-layout transfer is not masked by table-position strategies. |
| U1-L07 | elevated_monitor | Form fields, actions, months, and digital context meet in one realistic simulated-form task. | Five new lexical sets plus three recycles may make instructions feel like a vocabulary list. |
| U1-L08 | high_split_candidate | The profile capability naturally integrates family lexis, present facts, possession, quantity, noun phrases, and coordination. | Ten introductions and three recycles make this the strongest candidate for a two-lesson split. |
| U1-L09 | integration_no_new_items | No new exact item is introduced; the lesson tests message-purpose transfer across prior learning. | Check that success is not inflated by recognition-only message choices before fresh writing. |
| U2-L01 | standard_transition | The first unit transition reuses family language while adding people descriptions and bounded intelligibility contrasts. | Observe whether family content feels inclusive and whether learners infer personality from visuals despite instructions. |

## Important production decision

U1-L08 is the strongest split candidate, followed by U1-L05. They remain
single v1 lessons so the progression pilot can measure actual duration, fatigue,
and construct-specific failure. A split is required if their named triggers fire;
the 45-lesson count is not protected from evidence-led revision.
