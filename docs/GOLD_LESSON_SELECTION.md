# Gold Lesson Selection v1

Status: selected for authoring.

## Selection

The first gold lesson is `lesson_candidate.give_short_self_introduction`.

> In a fresh first-meeting situation, the learner can give a short
> self-introduction with essential personal details without a complete model.

## Why this candidate

- It has one observable A1 outcome and no hard prerequisite.
- It exercises production, intelligibility, transfer, and repair rather than recall.
- It supports safe fictional details, so testing does not require private disclosure.
- It can compare Arabic support, optional video, AI practice, retry, and teach-back.
- Its six source records cover identity, origin, residence, role, family or interest,
  and basic precision without forcing every example into one script.

## Boundary

The mastery task requires four small categories: name, origin, residence or role, and
one family or interest detail. It does not require a personal-detail interview,
introducing another person, writing a profile, or memorising a fixed paragraph.

## Evidence artifact

`gold/introduce-yourself/source-packet.json` contains the approved candidate, exact
source records, physical page locations, hashes, target decisions, error hypothesis,
and mastery contract used by the lesson.
