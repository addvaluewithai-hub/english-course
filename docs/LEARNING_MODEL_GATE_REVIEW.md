# Learning Model Gate Review: LEARN-004

Decision: pass after resolution of the independent critique. Phase 2 may open only for
the next queued source-record schema task; later phase gates remain locked.

## Review scope and method

The review compared the complete Phase 1 contract across:

- `docs/LEARNER_LANGUAGE_POLICY.md`;
- `docs/MASTERY_PROGRESS_POLICY.md`;
- `docs/AI_TEACHER_TEACHBACK_POLICY.md`;
- `docs/PRIVACY_ACCESSIBILITY_POLICY.md`;
- `docs/LEARNING_PRINCIPLES.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md`.

One read-only reviewer performed an independent contradiction and omission pass. The
primary agent checked each finding against the policies and cited legacy product briefs,
made the changes, and reran the gate checks.

## Resolved critique findings

| Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Teach-back was both mandatory and optional and appeared on both sides of mastery. | The test baseline now puts a matched optional teach-back variant after fresh performance and requires an omit variant. |
| Later teach-back could appear to supply a missing same-attempt mastery rating. | Demonstration is determined from fresh performance; teach-back is separate corroborating or review evidence only. |
| Early stop or safety pause could be reported as completion. | Ending outcomes are `completed`, `stopped incomplete`, or `safety-paused`; only the full required experience completes. |
| Final correction required an unauthored replacement task. | Each lesson now needs a primary and equivalent replacement variant or bounded mutation rules; one replacement is the initial cap. |
| Prototype data collection lacked minimum privacy boundaries. | Structured de-identified evidence is the default; raw capture requires a defined purpose, consent, access, deletion, and withdrawal plan. |
| A modality change could silently prove an easier proxy skill. | Permitted support must preserve the construct; construct-changing support is recorded as alternate or practice evidence. |
| Video had no defined Phase 1 role. | Video is an optional accessible medium whose added value is tested; watching never establishes mastery. |
| “Review until retained” could imply retention inside one sitting. | The core loop now distinguishes later review and delayed fresh retention probes. |

## Coherence checks

- **Learner and promise:** one practical, measurable ability for an Arabic-speaking adult
  beginner; completion, demonstration, and retention remain distinct.
- **Language:** English carries target input and performance; concise natural MSA removes
  essential barriers without becoming a translation track.
- **Lesson scope:** no fixed duration, unit count, video requirement, or permanent screen
  flow is locked before curriculum and learner evidence.
- **Mastery:** a fresh source-linked task, no complete model, and level 2 on every
  required dimension in the same performance; no averaging or proxy score.
- **AI behavior:** finite lesson-bounded turns, one important correction at a time,
  bounded retry, one replacement final attempt, and a definite ending.
- **Teach-back:** matched include/omit test variants after performance; separate evidence
  that cannot rescue a failed mastery task.
- **Safety and privacy:** fictional details, minimal de-identified evidence, no raw
  capture without a prior protocol and consent, and no mastery judgment during crisis.
- **Accessibility:** required media alternatives and controls are explicit; support may
  improve access but may not silently change the assessed ability.

## Remaining test hypotheses

Teach-back value and fatigue, video value, Arabic amount, bounded retry sufficiency,
retention timing, task-variant equivalence, and evaluator agreement still require gold
lesson or learner-test evidence. They are explicitly testable and do not contradict the
baseline shape, so they do not block the learning-model gate.
