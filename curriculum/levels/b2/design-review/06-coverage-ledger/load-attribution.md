## Why the raw counts do not directly equal learner teaching load

A source row is a coverage/evidence obligation, not automatically an independently foregrounded teaching object. This matters especially before Stage 8 because B2 lexical, phrase and grammar records have not yet received final productive/receptive/formulaic/integrated-support roles.

A lesson with 28 lexical source rows cannot be authored as “teach 28 words productively.” The Stage-4 policy explicitly allows an active core plus supported recognition/contextual exposure, and Stage 8 must decide the exact role record by record. Likewise, 21 EGP rows may represent a materially smaller number of teachable grammar systems/functions when rows belong to one bounded semantic system.
