# Stage-2 phrase → Stage-3 grammar reconciliation

Stage 2 deliberately flagged fixed phrases that could otherwise create grammar overreach. Stage 3 resolves them as follows.

### Directly grammar-backed within bounded sourced functions

- `as long as…` → G12/G13: conditional/subordinator use only.
- `as though…` → G07/G13: comparison/subordinator use only.
- `bound to do sth` → G24: certainty/inevitability branch.
- `despite the fact that…` → G13: complex subordinator branch.
- `be known to do sth` → cross-link G32 where passive infinitive/impersonal construction is supported.
- `if only…` → G35: past-perfect regret branch.
- `one another` → G42: reciprocal-pronoun use.
- `regarded as sth` → cross-link G32 only as a bounded passive/reporting pattern.

### Grammar interface exists, but phrase remains bounded pending exact semantic/control reconciliation

- `be expected to do sth` → passive/adjectival expectation interface; no phrase-led broad passive or modality claim.
- `ever since` → tense/time interface; no unrestricted perfect-system claim from the phrase alone.
- `find yourself doing sth` → complementation/reflexive interface; exact lexical pattern remains bounded.
- `go on to do sth` → G46 complementation interface; meaning contrast must remain explicit.
- `in spite of sth` → G38 complex-preposition interface; does not license a clause pattern by itself.
- `prove to be sth` → G47 linking/complement interface; exact lexical behavior remains source-linked.
- `so as to do sth` → purpose/non-finite interface; do not generalize to all infinitive purpose structures from the phrase alone.
- `subject to sth` → G38 formal preposition/adjectival interface; keep exact sense/register.
- `whether or not…` → subordination/discourse interface; no broader conditional claim without exact EGP evidence.

### Remain phrase/formulaic evidence; no direct B2 EGP system license found in this review

- `had better`
- `I have to say…`
- `I would say…`
- `in an attempt to do sth`
- `no doubt`
- `no matter…`
- `would rather…`

These can still be important B2 phrases, but Stage 3 does not promote them into broader grammar systems merely because their surface form looks grammatical.
