# Level Source of Truth v1

This directory is the curriculum foundation for A1-C2. Each level owns a reviewed source inventory and cumulative exit contract before lesson design is authorized.

## Start here

For the reusable A2-C2 design process, including the manual review sequence, critic gates, lifecycle ledger, semantic-lock reconciliation, CI requirements, and new-chat handoff instructions, read:

- `docs/LEVEL_CURRICULUM_DESIGN_PLAYBOOK_V1.md`
- `docs/LEVEL_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_STATUS_V1.md`

A1 is the completed reference implementation of that process. Later levels should copy the **method**, not A1's unit count, lesson count, capability count, or productive/receptive proportions.

## Rules

- Sources are evidence; the reviewed level files are the course source of truth.
- Level inventories represent items newly assigned at that level; exit contracts are cumulative through the level.
- Lessons consume inventory items. They do not invent required level scope.
- Every included item must retain source provenance and a review status.
- Exclusions and deferrals must be explicit.
- Completion/exposure is not mastery; retention needs later fresh evidence.
- Mastery/retention requirements belong in `exit_contract.json` and the level lifecycle/coverage ledger.
- Curriculum lesson-design authorization is separate from delivery/media-production authorization.
- The existing delivery pilot and `TEST-002` are not changed by this curriculum workstream unless explicitly authorized.

## Current source roles

- Words: Oxford 3000/5000 through C1; Pearson GSE Vocabulary C2 General Learner for C2.
- Phrases: Oxford Phrase List through C1; Pearson GSE Vocabulary phrases/phrasal verbs and attached collocations for C2.
- Grammar: English Grammar Profile as canonical inventory, cross-checked with Pearson GSE Grammar.
- Abilities: CEFR Companion Volume and Pearson GSE Adult General English objectives through reviewed granular assignments.
- Pronunciation: reviewed course pronunciation progression strands plus level-performance evidence.

C2 Pearson-derived lexical content retains `commercial_permission_required`; curriculum architecture may proceed without implying commercial licensing clearance.
