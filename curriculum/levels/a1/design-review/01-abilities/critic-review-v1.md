# A1 Capability Map v1 — Manual Critic Review

Status: **CRITIC COMPLETE — REVISIONS REQUIRED BEFORE LOCK**

Basis:
- `manual-review-log.md` — manual disposition of all 119 required A1 abilities.
- `capability-map-v1.md` — 27 candidate capability consolidation.
- `critic-notes.md` — open critic questions.

This review does not use the earlier automated cluster/content-bank artifacts and does not use the old lesson sequence as a design constraint.

## Executive conclusion

The 27-capability map is substantially better than the source list, but it is still structurally uneven. Some entries are genuine real-world capabilities, while others are semantic/grammatical functions, lexical contexts, or cross-cutting interaction mechanics. The map should not be locked as-is.

The critic recommends a **21-capability candidate structure** before phrase review. This is not a target number; it is the consequence of the specific merges/demotions/split below. Phrase and grammar review may still force later revision.

## Critic decisions

### 1. MERGE C01 + C02

Current:
- C01 Basic social contact and wellbeing
- C02 Meet and introduce people

Recommended family:
- **Meet, greet, and introduce people**

Reason:
Greeting, responding socially, introductions, wellbeing, and leave-taking form one coherent first-contact interaction. They share a single real-world encounter and naturally recycle together. Keep mandatory branches for greeting, wellbeing, self-introduction, introducing another person, responding to an introduction, reacting simply, and leave-taking.

Do **not** absorb C03. Exchanging personal details can occur outside a first-meeting script and includes locating/understanding details across modalities.

### 2. KEEP C03, C04, C05 distinct for now

Current:
- C03 Exchange basic personal information
- C04 Exchange information about family and friends
- C05 Exchange information about home and residence

Decision: **KEEP as separate candidate capabilities through phrase/grammar review.**

Reason:
These domains will likely carry materially different phrase, grammar, and lexical loads. Merging them now would create a large “talk about yourself” bucket that is hard to audit and easy to under-assess. Phrase/grammar evidence may later justify a family grouping, but each domain must remain visible.

### 3. DEMOTE C06 from top-level capability

Current:
- C06 Talk about possessions and ownership

Decision: move to **cross-cutting semantic requirement: possession/ownership**.

Reason:
“Have/possess/belong to” is a semantic/grammatical function used inside personal information, family, home, and object tasks. It does not need its own real-world curriculum endpoint. Preserve all source rows and require the function to appear in multiple relevant capabilities.

### 4. KEEP C07, C08, C09 distinct

Current:
- preferences/interests
- routines/free time
- ability/inability

Decision: **KEEP separate.**

Reason:
They overlap thematically but have different communicative meanings and likely different grammar/phrase systems: preference, habitual activity, and ability are not interchangeable. Combining them would create a generic “talk about activities” bucket and weaken assessment precision.

### 5. KEEP C10, C11, C12 with a warning

Current:
- understand simple descriptions
- exchange information about objects
- talk about location and position

Decision: **KEEP provisionally**, but phrase/grammar review must test whether C10 becomes too generic.

Reason:
C10 is receptive and cross-domain; C11 is interactive/productive about object properties; C12 is spatial information. They are not duplicates. However, if C10 ends up merely reusing language from domain capabilities without its own meaningful assessment task, it should later become a receptive cross-cutting requirement rather than a top-level capability.

### 6. DEMOTE C13 weather

Current:
- C13 Describe basic weather

Decision: move to **mandatory content context**, not top-level capability.

Reason:
The source objective is real, but “weather” is too narrow to justify equal architectural status with directions, transactions, forms, or personal interaction. It is a natural lexical/contextual branch under description/everyday information and may also support routines/plans. It must remain traceable and assessed, but not as its own top-level capability.

### 7. SPLIT C14 into two capability families

Current:
- C14 Handle basic numbers, quantities, times and dates

Recommended:
- **Handle basic numbers and quantities**
- **Handle basic time and dates**

Reason:
The learning and assessment tasks differ materially. Number/quantity work includes cardinal numbers, number strings, phone numbers, and `how many`; time/date work includes clock time, days/months, dates, and time references. They may share numeral knowledge, but they behave differently in phrases, grammar, and tasks. Prices should remain cross-linked to service transactions rather than being owned by the generic number capability.

### 8. KEEP C15 and C16

Current:
- understand simple arrangements/event details
- understand basic signs/notices

Decision: **KEEP.**

Reason:
Both are distinct functional-literacy/comprehension tasks with clear success conditions. C15 uses time/place language but is not equivalent to “know time and dates.” C16 is a real public-reading task rather than generic reading comprehension.

### 9. KEEP C17 and C18 separate

Current:
- follow simple instructions/procedures
- ask for and follow simple directions

Decision: **KEEP separate.**

Reason:
Procedural/command language and route navigation have materially different language, task structure, and assessment. The shared word “instructions” is not enough reason to merge them.

### 10. TIGHTEN C19

Current:
- manage basic communication problems: get attention + ask for spelling/written form

Recommended:
- **Repair a basic communication problem**

Own:
- ask for spelling;
- ask for a word to be written down;
- later phrase review may add simple repetition/clarification language if source-supported.

Move “get someone’s attention” to the social/request system rather than treating it as repair.

Reason:
Getting attention is interaction management, while spelling/written-form requests are repair strategies. The current capability mixes two different purposes.

### 11. DEMOTE C20

Current:
- C20 Ask for basic information

Decision: move to **cross-cutting interaction requirement: initiate basic questions**.

Reason:
The map already contains concrete question-asking tasks in personal information, objects, quantities, directions, etc. A generic “ask for information” top-level capability duplicates them. The important guarantee is that learners initiate questions, not only answer them; that should be enforced across relevant capabilities.

### 12. KEEP C21

Current:
- express immediate needs; make/respond to requests/offers

Decision: **KEEP**, with mandatory branches.

Reason:
This is a coherent everyday interaction function. Wants/needs, basic requests, help, and accepting/responding to offers are related enough at A1 to form one family, provided each branch remains visible.

### 13. MERGE C22–C25 into one transaction family with mandatory branches

Current:
- C22 prices/shopping
- C23 food/drink ordering
- C24 transport ticket
- C25 hotel check-in

Recommended:
- **Handle basic service transactions**

Mandatory branches:
1. simple shopping/price exchange;
2. order basic food/drink;
3. buy a basic public-transport ticket;
4. complete a very basic hotel check-in.

Reason:
These share the same macro-purpose: complete a predictable service transaction using fixed/basic language and required information. Keeping four top-level capabilities over-fragments the architecture. However, the merge is valid only because all four branches remain mandatory and separately traceable/assessable; one scenario must never stand in for the others.

### 14. KEEP C26 and C27

Current:
- complete basic forms
- write a simple personal message

Decision: **KEEP separate.**

Reason:
These are distinct writing tasks with different products, conventions, and success criteria. They should not disappear into generic personal-information or writing buckets.

## Cross-cutting requirements after critic

The following remain required but should not be top-level capabilities:
- possession/ownership;
- weather as a mandatory lexical/content context;
- initiate basic information questions in relevant capabilities;
- understand common basic questions in relevant capabilities;
- slow/clear/short/familiar A1 input conditions where source-evidenced;
- visual support where source-evidenced;
- spoken repetition/read-aloud/intelligibility support;
- spelling, capitalisation, punctuation, and copying requirements;
- getting attention as interaction management/social-request language.

## Risks still open for Stage 02/03

1. C08 (`understand simple descriptions`) may still be too generic. Phrase and grammar mapping must prove that it has a meaningful language/task identity; otherwise demote it to a receptive cross-cutting requirement.
2. C03/C04/C05 may later become one higher-level family with mandatory subdomains if phrase/grammar mapping shows they overwhelmingly share the same language architecture. Do not merge yet.
3. The service-transaction family must preserve all four scenarios as mandatory branches. If later mapping shows materially different grammar/phrase systems, it can be split again.
4. Phrase review may expose missing capability functions not visible in the source ability wording. The capability map is allowed to change if the evidence demands it.

## Critic verdict

**Do not lock the 27-capability v1 map.**

Advance a **21-capability v2 candidate** into manual phrase review, with the cross-cutting requirements above kept explicit and traceable. Stage 01 should be marked `REVISED / READY FOR V2`, not `LOCKED`, until the v2 file is written and checked against all 119 source abilities.
