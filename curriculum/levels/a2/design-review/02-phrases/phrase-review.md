# Stage-02 critic decisions

1. **No new top-level capability is justified by phrase evidence.** Every phrase has a natural capability or cross-cutting home without force-fitting a new learner-performance family.
2. **X06 is substantially strengthened.** Twenty-nine A2 phrases support time-linking, sequence, addition, cause/result, reason, contrast and simple relation-building. This confirms A2's exit promise of linked simple language and must shape lesson design later.
3. **Do not create grammar from phrase strings.** `Have you ever…?`, `have got to`, `be allowed to`, `as soon as`, `if necessary`, `so that`, `would love to`, comparison-looking chunks and similar forms are explicit Stage-03 flags, not permission to invent a grammar syllabus now.
4. **C19 health remains justified.** Six phrases reinforce the already distinct form/advice/symptom survival task rather than merely supplying a topic word set.
5. **C26 remains provisional, not promoted.** Its nine phrase homes are mostly contextual nouns/frames (`art gallery`, `climate change`, `police station`, etc.). Words must show whether there is enough coherent language to support a real familiar-place/culture performance sequence; otherwise C26 may later be folded into domain branches plus X02.
6. **C23 remains narrow and under observation.** Phrase density here is informational language, but a phrase list cannot by itself prove a meaningful standalone comprehension unit. The ability boundary remains valid for now.
7. **Low phrase count does not demote ability-driven capabilities.** C03 phone, C15 plans/predictions and C24 interviews/surveys have little/no direct Oxford phrase ownership, but their source abilities define real distinct performances; grammar/words/carry-forward A1 language may support them.
8. **Polysemy must remain bounded.** `take off`, `work out`, `deal with`, `depend on` and similar chunks must be taught in the sense supported by the chosen task/source context; no all-senses mastery claim.
9. **Phrase home still does not equal productive exit role.** Stage 08 will decide productive/formulaic/receptive control after the instructional architecture exists.
