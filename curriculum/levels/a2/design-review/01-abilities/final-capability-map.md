# Capability architecture

## C01 — Take part in simple social contact and small talk

**Source-review rows:** 45, 46, 50, 82, 103, 156, 158, 183, 208.  
**Boundary:** short face-to-face social exchange: open, maintain briefly, respond, and close. Small-talk language supports a real exchange; it is not a memorised list of greetings. Broader pragmatic reactions live in X05.

## C02 — Exchange and record personal, family, education and work background information

**Source-review rows:** 9, 16, 38, 87, 112, 120, 121, 157, 206, 215, 221, 222, 234, 239, 254, 263.  
**Boundary:** personal/background information across speech and writing, including family, living conditions, education, job and basic profile data. Do not collapse later past-experience narration into this capability merely because it is autobiographical.

## C03 — Handle simple phone calls and phone messages

**Source-review rows:** 51, 52, 68, 80, 113, 141, 162, 172, 252.  
**Boundary:** channel-specific mechanics: identify recipient, introduce/close, answer simply, leave/take a message and use repair while taking it. Booking/cancelling/appointments remain C11 because the transaction outcome, not the phone channel, is primary.

## C04 — Describe and understand people’s appearance, personality and feelings

**Source-review rows:** 2, 41, 89, 128, 173, 186, 203, 230, 240.  
**Boundary:** appearance/clothes, personality and feelings are related description branches but must remain visibly assessable; emotional/social reactions such as apology, encouragement or regret are X05 rather than evidence that the learner can describe feelings.

## C06 — Express and understand preferences, simple opinions, agreement/disagreement and brief reasons

# Stage-01 critic decisions

The following decisions were made after reading all 269 rows and challenging the first-pass grouping.

1. **Do not copy A1's capability count or transaction structure.** A2 evidence independently yields 26 top-level families plus 8 cross-cutting requirements; the count is an outcome, not a target.
2. **Keep targeted practical-information retrieval (C22) distinct from general short-text comprehension (C23).** “Find the time/price/fact” and “understand the main point/key details” have different success conditions.
3. **Keep factual past experience (C16) distinct from narrative sequencing/story (C17).** Similar past-time content does not make the performances identical.
4. **Keep invitations/arrangements (C14) distinct from individual plans/intentions/predictions (C15).** Coordination with another person/time/place is an assessment boundary.
5. **Keep phone mechanics (C03) distinct from reservation/travel outcomes (C11).** A phone reservation retrieves both, but one cannot substitute for the other.
6. **Keep directions/navigation (C10) distinct from static object/location description (C08).** Route movement and spatial description remain different performances.
7. **Keep skills/ability (C25) separate from request/permission (C12).** Shared modal forms must not collapse communicative function.
8. **Do not make apology, compliment, encouragement, interjections, etc. separate capabilities.** X05 preserves them as mandatory pragmatic resources inside meaningful interaction.
9. **Do not make generic “talk about familiar topics” rows into a giant capability.** X01/X02 preserve level conditions and linked-output development without creating a meaningless catch-all.
10. **Do not turn two rehearsed-announcement rows into a full unit architecture.** X08 remains mandatory but cross-cutting pending phrase/pronunciation evidence.
11. **Medical communication remains narrow but top-level (C19).** It has receptive advice/form and productive symptom/form branches with a clear survival-task success condition; it is not merely a lexical topic.
12. **No ability row is structurally split at Stage 01.** Broad rows such as background domains, personality/emotions, or arrangement details are retained as mandatory sub-branches inside one coherent performance. Later phrase/grammar evidence may reopen this if it reveals materially different systems.
13. **Reception/production/interaction boundaries remain visible inside every merged family.** MERGE means shared capability architecture, not permission to assess a receptive row with speaking evidence or vice versa.
14. **Previous locked A1 remains prerequisite, not A2-new content.** Carry-forward/retrieval mapping is deferred to Stage 4.5 and must not silently edit A1.

## Open risks for Stage 02/03

- C09 may later need stronger mandatory service branches if phrase/grammar evidence shows materially different transaction systems.
- C23 is intentionally narrow; if phrase/grammar/content evidence shows it is only a generic receptive condition rather than a meaningful task family, it may be demoted to cross-cutting reception support.
- C26 has only three direct ability rows but a clear “immediate world” exit function; words/phrases must prove it has enough coherent instructional content to stay top-level.
- X08 may acquire a dedicated lesson slot later, but that would not automatically make it a top-level capability.
- A2's much larger grammar inventory (291 EGP rows) is expected to change dependency ordering and may expose capability boundaries that ability wording alone cannot show.
