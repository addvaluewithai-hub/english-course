# A2 Curriculum Load & Sequence Lock

Status: **MILESTONE 2 COMPLETE — 12 UNITS / 83 LESSON SLOTS RETAINED**  
Scope: human cognitive-load, branch-coherence and sequence critic after exact Stage-06 coverage.  
This file locks curriculum scheduling only. It does **not** assign final productive/receptive/formulaic/embedded semantic roles and does not authorize lesson production.

## Final structural decision

The Stage-05 skeleton remains **12 units / 83 lesson slots**.

The human critic found no pedagogical reason to add, remove, merge or split a lesson after the source rows were routed by actual lesson performance rather than mechanically round-robin inside broad capability families.

The 83 count is therefore now a **load-and-sequence decision**, not merely the Stage-05 baseline.

## What the critic tested

The review used the curriculum map plus the exact generated lesson-introduction attribution and challenged all lesson slots for:

- one coherent primary communicative/evidence outcome;
- branch fit inside multi-branch capabilities;
- accidental topic/lexical dumping;
- accidental grammar dumping caused by raw EGP row counts;
- sequencing of prerequisite form/function before transfer;
- late introductions that would lose practice or fresh retrieval;
- new-content leakage into transfer/evidence lessons;
- arbitrary `LX-GENERAL` vocabulary entering a lesson merely because capacity existed;
- separation of superficially similar but assessably different performances.

Raw source-row count was treated as a mechanical warning, not as a teaching-object count.

## Major human routing corrections

### Unit 1 — contact, background and phone

- C02 was separated into own/background profile versus asking about another person's background.
- C03 was explicitly separated into **basic phone-call mechanics** (`U1-L04`) versus **phone-message taking/leaving and phone repair** (`U1-L05`).
- C20 repair rows were routed to phone-message support or the dedicated repair lesson according to actual function.
- C01/X01/X05 were redistributed so `U1-L01` remains social contact rather than a dump of all A2 interaction conditions.
- `U1-L04` was removed from the `LX-GENERAL` introduction window. Final load is **5 abilities + 2 pronunciation requirements and zero new vocabulary/phrases/grammar**.

### Unit 2 — people and everyday lives

- appearance/clothing versus personality/feelings were routed to separate lessons;
- routines/frequency, preferences/hobbies, work/study activity and skills/ability were routed to their intended performances;
- generic activity rows were moved where they support the actual task instead of forcing U2-L03/U2-L04 over the ability guardrail.

### Unit 3 — description and comparison

- home/room description, object/use/ownership, direct comparison, quantity/possession comparison and reference-building were kept distinct;
- reference grammar was distributed across profile/description/experience/media contexts rather than concentrated as a 55-row grammar lesson;
- `U3-L04` may introduce superlative grammar without a new ability row because it practises the already established C07 comparison performance rather than inventing a new capability.

### Unit 4 — social action

The human routing clarified the intended branch boundaries:

- `U4-L01`: immediate requests, borrowing/lending and direct provision;
- `U4-L02`: offers/declines, polite refusal, permission plus bounded obligation support;
- `U4-L03`: opinion/agreement/disagreement plus brief reason/contrast;
- `U4-L04`: collaborative suggestions;
- `U4-L05`: advice;
- `U4-L06`: apology/excuse/reaction/encouragement plus clarification/check-understanding support.

Social-reaction phrases such as `I don't care`, `no way` and `Oh well` were removed from the email lesson and placed in the pragmatic social-action sequence.

### Unit 5 — routine goods/services

- quantity, product comparison, food ordering, purchase, other routine service and practical-information retrieval retain separate success conditions;
- X01 routine-exchange conditions and C07 product comparison now receive later evidence in the fresh U5 transfer rather than becoming late lifecycle gaps.

### Unit 6 — directions/travel/reservations

C11 rows were explicitly routed by outcome:

- travel status/timetable information;
- ticket/tourist-information exchange;
- accommodation details;
- phone reservation/change/cancel/appointment.

Phone is only the channel in the final branch; C03 phone mechanics remain separately assessable.

### Unit 7 — invitations, arrangements and future

- invitation response, coordinated arrangements, diary/calendar recording, individual intentions, scheduled/arranged future and prediction/possibility remain separate branches;
- future grammar was kept distributed by communicative function rather than collapsed into a generic future-tense chapter;
- the existing seven-slot unit remains sufficient.

### Units 8–9 — past experience versus narrative

- factual past/personal experience rows were realigned across past statement, past question, present-perfect experience and experience-profile lessons;
- narrative rows were realigned across background event, sequence comprehension, story comprehension and actual story production;
- receptive narrative evidence was removed from the story-production slot where round-robin scheduling had placed it.

This confirms the architectural decision to keep C16 factual experience distinct from C17 narrative sequencing.

### Unit 10 — instructions and health

- safety/rules, everyday process and equipment/procedure instructions are separately routed;
- symptoms versus medical advice/form completion are separately routed;
- advice/repair in `U10-L06` is intentionally mostly reuse rather than another new-language lesson.

### Unit 11 — writing, information and media

- personal correspondence versus written information request/reply are separated;
- C22 practical scanning rows were routed to service/travel/general practical-material contexts rather than one generic reading dump;
- factual-media comprehension keeps reference/reporting infrastructure where it can be encountered inside supported short material;
- survey/interview task-format rows are consolidated in `U11-L06`.

`U11-L01` and `U11-L04` remain high mechanical-load lessons, but the critic retained them because their rows describe a small number of coherent instructional systems rather than 43/45 independent teaching objects. Stage 08/8.5 must still make those systems role-aware before authoring.

### Unit 12 — local-world integration and exit

- school/workplace/home-town description was separated from cultural-information output;
- broad environment/science/society/culture vocabulary was moved earlier where it can be processed in factual/inference/local contexts;
- `U12-L03` is now a true rehearsed-delivery lesson: **2 X08 abilities, zero new words, zero new phrases, zero new grammar**;
- `U12-L04`–`U12-L07` remain zero-new evidence/integration slots.

## High-load lesson decisions

The final exact ledger has no mechanical threshold violations. The highest total introduction loads are:

- `U4-L03`: **45** source records;
- `U11-L04`: **45** source records;
- `U1-L03`: **44** source records;
- `U11-L01`: **43** source records;
- `U3-L02` and `U10-L02`: **42** source records.

These were reviewed rather than automatically split.

Reasons for retaining them:

- multiple EGP rows often describe one bounded grammar/reference system;
- ability rows often express modality/evidence variants of the same learner performance;
- lexical rows may be supported recognition/context rather than independent active targets;
- the final semantic role is deliberately deferred to Milestone 3 / Stage 08.

If Stage 08 later classifies an unexpectedly large proportion of one of these lessons as independently productive, Stage 08.5 must reopen the load decision. The present lock is therefore stable but evidence-responsive, not immutable dogma.
