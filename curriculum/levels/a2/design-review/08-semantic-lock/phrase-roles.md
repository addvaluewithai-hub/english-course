## Critic notes

1. C22/C23 phrases are not promoted to active vocabulary just because they appear in informational lessons.
2. C26 content phrases remain contextual; `climate change`, `in prison`, `police officer`, etc. do not create an active world-topics syllabus.
3. X06 is a **repertoire requirement**, not 29 independent exit items.
4. Polysemous phrases remain sense-bounded to their Stage-02 home.
5. `Have you ever…?`, `have got to`, `would love to`, `be allowed to`, `if necessary`, etc. remain formulaic phrase claims here; broader grammar control is owned separately by Stage-03 systems.
