## Semantic critic decisions

1. **Formulaic phrase evidence does not expand grammar.** Phrase chunks and grammar systems remain separate contracts.
2. **Reference infrastructure is not a hidden grammar exam.** X09 is evidenced through coherent linked output/comprehension.
3. **Passive and reported speech are deliberately not promoted to independent productive exit mastery.** Their A2 evidence is better represented through supported factual/process input and embedded use.
4. **Question/modality/future systems remain function-split.** Shared surface form cannot substitute for request, permission, advice, arrangement, prediction, ability or repair success.
5. **Past systems remain performance-split.** Past simple/present perfect experience (C16) and past-continuous narrative background (C17) are not interchangeable evidence.
6. **Mechanical row load is now semantically compressed.** A lesson containing many rows from one productive/support system is not treated as if every row were an independent target.
