## A. Productive contextual lexical core — 591

`mastery_mode=productive`, `control_scope=contextual`, `exit_evidence=integrated_required`.

A productive word must be usable in a fresh A2 task inside its reviewed sense/home. This does **not** mean every productive word must be separately forced at exit.

The productive core was selected where the lexical item materially supports one or more of:

- personal/background, routines, preferences, appearance/feelings and linked description;
- home/object/position/comparison language;
- shopping/food/quantity/service transactions;
- directions/travel/reservation outcomes;
- requests/offers/permission/advice/suggestions/arrangements;
- past experience/narrative sequencing;
- instructions/process/health survival communication;
- repair/correspondence/interview task language;
- high-utility description, time, quantity, action and evaluation language needed to make fresh A2 output possible.

Examples of productive decisions include `accept`, `arrange`, `available`, `borrow`, `background`, `cash`, `colleague`, `comfortable`, `disagree`, `distance`, `experience`, `headache`, `lend`, `permission`, `predict`, `recommend`, `route`, `salary`, `schedule`, `suggest`, `survey`, `worried` and many common descriptive/action words.

## B. Integrated grammar/discourse/reference support — 54

`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint` or `bounded_pattern`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

These exact word records are not independent lexical targets. They are controlled through Stage-03 grammar/reference/discourse systems or linked-output quality. This bucket includes:

- the full reviewed **L29 grammar-owned set (23 records)**, including `have` auxiliary, `may`, `might`, `shall`, possessive/reflexive pronouns, `whose`, and related function/reference items;
- connectors/reference/discourse items such as `according to`, `actually`, `although`, `as`, `after`, `before`, `instead`, `finally`, `firstly`, `fortunately`, `unfortunately`, `since`, `while`, `yet` where the semantic obligation is better represented through X06/X09/grammar control than through vocabulary recall.

These items can of course appear in learner output; the distinction is that lexical mastery is not independently claimed apart from the owning grammar/discourse system.

## C. Receptive/contextual vocabulary — 226

`mastery_mode=receptive`, `control_scope=contextual`, `exit_evidence=integrated_required`.

These words broaden A2 comprehension and content range but are not required as independent fresh output. Major groups include:

- broad environment/science/society/public-life content;
- many profession, sport, animal, culture/history and media labels beyond what a learner personally needs to produce;
- crime/law/public-authority content mainly carried by factual/practical input;
- lower-necessity factual nouns/verbs that make C22/C23 texts richer without creating an active topic syllabus;
- exact homograph/sense records whose productive need was not established by the locked A2 performances.

Examples include `advertising`, `architecture`, `army`, `biology`, `celebrity`, `continent`, `crime`, `data`, `disaster`, `electricity`, `environment`, `government`, `industry`, `pollution`, `president`, `society`, `war` and similar broad factual content.

## Important semantic boundaries

1. **Productive is contextual, not unrestricted.** A word is active only in the reviewed A2 sense(s); homographs remain exact-record specific.
2. **Receptive does not mean unimportant.** Receptive words can be required for main-point/detail/information-retrieval success and must receive repeated input exposure.
3. **Grammar-owned words are not double-counted.** Their active form/use obligation is represented by the grammar/reference system.
4. **Topic breadth is not output breadth.** A2 can understand a wider world than it can productively discuss.
5. **No `LX-GENERAL` default became active automatically.** Each productive general-remainder item was selected manually; the rest remain receptive.
6. **Exit evidence is integrated.** The course does not promise a 591-item decontextualised production test.
