# A2 Pronunciation Semantic Roles

Status: **HUMAN REVIEW COMPLETE — 10 / 10 STRANDS**  
Source constraint review: `../04.5-pronunciation-exit-carry-forward/constraints.md`

Pronunciation is a distributed intelligibility/perception contract. It is not a standalone capability and does not impose accent imitation or B1 fluency.

## P01 — Sound perception and familiar-word recognition
`mastery_mode=receptive`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Recognise selected contrasts/familiar words across routine voices and generally clear delivery.

## P02 — Consonant clarity
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Maintain selected consonant contrasts well enough that routine meaning is normally recoverable.

## P03 — Vowel clarity and common unstressed vowels
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Selected vowel contrasts and common unstressed-vowel recognition/production are judged through intelligibility, not accent sameness.

## P04 — Syllable shape, clusters and word endings
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Common cluster/endings must not be reduced/expanded so severely that routine understanding breaks down.

## P05 — Audible grammatical endings
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Common `-s` / `-ed` contrasts must be sufficiently audible/recognisable inside routines, description and past-event tasks.

## P06 — Word stress
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Expected stress across a wider routine A2 repertoire; no isolated pronunciation exam is required.

## P07 — Sentence prominence and rhythm
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

A2 introduction/practice target: highlight key content words and reduce selected predictable function words in short routine utterances.

## P08 — Connected-speech perception and production
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Understand common routine linking/reductions and use selected connected forms without losing important words.

## P09 — Intonation, interaction and stance
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Use/recognise basic contours for statements, questions, continuation, politeness and turn-taking.

## P10 — Chunking, pausing, monitoring and pronunciation repair
`mastery_mode=integrated_support`, `control_scope=quality_constraint`, `exit_evidence=support_condition`.

Group short language into clear units and use repetition/slower delivery/rewording when communication needs repair.

## Exit boundary

A2 speech must be **generally understandable in familiar situations**. Repetition, hesitation, listener effort, false starts and reformulation may still occur. The course does not claim native-like accent, automatic connected speech or B1-level fluency.
