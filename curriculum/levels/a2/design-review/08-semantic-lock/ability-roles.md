## Critic decisions

1. Source reception is never promoted to production for convenience.
2. Source interaction is not reduced to one-way spoken production; turn success matters.
3. Fixed-expression wording narrows scope to formulaic rather than weakening the required communicative outcome.
4. Cross-cutting rows remain mandatory evidence/conditions but do not become fake independent capabilities.
5. A learner may still make systematic basic mistakes at A2 when meaning remains clear; semantic lock must not silently impose B1 accuracy.
