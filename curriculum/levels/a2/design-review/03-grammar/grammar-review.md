# Architecture change from Stage 03

## Add X09 — Reference-building and noun-phrase expansion

Grammar rows **67–74, 83–99, 179–193, 231–259** (with supporting cross-links from G36/G46) make reference tracking too important to leave implicit.

**X09 requirement:** learners must increasingly build and track reference in linked simple language using bounded relative clauses, demonstratives/back-reference, possessive and substitute pronouns, expanded noun phrases, indefinite/quantity reference and related prepositional structure.

**Why cross-cutting, not a capability:** reference-building has no independent real-world endpoint. It makes C02/C04/C05/C08/C21/C23/C26 more coherent and less repetitive. It must be practised inside those performances.

Architecture after Stage 03: **26 top-level capabilities + 9 cross-cutting requirements**.

# Stage-03 critic decisions

1. **Comparison is unquestionably a real A2 capability, not a lexical side effect.** G02/G04/G08 plus quantity grammar give C07 a substantial canonical system. Keep its spoken/written/receptive branches visible.
2. **C16 and C17 must remain separate.** Past simple + present perfect experiences serve factual life/event exchange; past continuous/background + sequencing serve narrative organisation. One “past tense unit” would erase a performance boundary.
3. **Future architecture is function-led, not tense-led.** `will/shall`, `be going to`, present continuous future, present simple timetable, limited future continuous and possibility modals distribute across C12/C13/C14/C15; do not create five tense chapters.
4. **C12 request/permission and C13 advice/suggestion remain distinct despite shared modals.** `can/could/will/would/shall/should` overlap formally but success conditions differ.
5. **C18 instruction/process is strongly validated.** Imperatives, ordered multi-clause instructions, real-condition hedging and present-simple instruction use form a coherent system.
6. **X04 must be strengthened.** A2 question formation now spans present/past/present perfect/continuous/modal/alternative/tag/indirect forms. Questions are cross-capability infrastructure, not a generic “questions” capability.
7. **X06 is again validated.** Linking/stance/sequencing adverbs, subordination, conditionals, fronting and discourse markers explain why Stage 02 found 29 cohesion-related phrases. Linked A2 output must be designed deliberately.
8. **Add X09, but do not create a noun-phrase/relative-clause capability.** Grammar control is infrastructure for description/reference, not a real-world outcome.
9. **Passive voice stays embedded.** It supports simple factual/process comprehension/production; source evidence does not justify a standalone passive syllabus.
10. **Reported speech is a Stage-4.5 flag, not yet a new capability.** It may support A2 mediation/relay requirements in the exit contract, but we will decide that only when pronunciation + target-exit + A1 carry-forward are reviewed together.
11. **C23 survives grammar critic.** Passive, relative/reference and reporting-clause systems give short factual/descriptive information a coherent language base; it remains separate from targeted scanning C22.
12. **C26 remains provisional.** Grammar does not independently strengthen the “local environment/culture” family enough to settle it; the 871-word review must decide whether it has a natural instructional spine or should be folded into other descriptions plus content contexts.
13. **No phrase-form overclaim survived unchecked.** `Have you ever…?`, `have got to`, `would love to`, real `if` clauses and broader comparison now have explicit canonical grammar support; fixed chunks without corresponding broad EGP support remain bounded.
14. **291 EGP rows must never become 291 explanations.** The 46 reviewed systems are themselves not automatically 46 lessons; several are infrastructure reused inside one communicative lesson.
