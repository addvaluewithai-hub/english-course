### Architecture relationship

This is cross-cutting rather than a standalone capability because it supports several existing performance families:

- C01/C03 — interpret reported/source-based complex input;
- C06/C10 — build evidence-aware positions and evaluations;
- C08/C09 — transform and summarise source material while preserving stance;
- C11 — professional/formal reporting.

Stage 3 must reconcile this refinement with reporting-passive, stance, modality and evidential grammar. Stage 4 must preserve lexical/phrase sense boundaries. Stage 8 will decide final control/mastery roles.
