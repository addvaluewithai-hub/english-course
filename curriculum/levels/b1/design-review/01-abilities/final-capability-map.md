# Cross-cutting mandatory requirements

These are required B1 source abilities/conditions but are deliberately **not** standalone top-level capabilities.

## X01 — Input-speed, clarity and repetition boundary

**Source-review rows:** 72.  
B1 can extend into rapid or longer familiar speech, but repetition or clarification may still be necessary. This condition constrains reception/interaction evidence and must not disappear merely because the level targets greater independence.

## X02 — Main-point hierarchy, emphasis and explanatory relationships

**Source-review rows:** 9, 16, 17, 47, 57, 93, 99, 191, 199, 237.

These are discourse-quality resources across C02/C05/C10/C11/C16/C18/C19, not a topicless standalone capability.

## X03 — Cohesion, chronology, paragraphing and basic text organisation

**Source-review rows:** 21, 22, 63, 184, 185, 189, 194, 203, 208, 218, 219, 221, 240.  
B1 output/reception must manage linear sequence, paragraph breaks, endings/conclusions, simple cohesive links, chronology and a basic counter-argument marker. These requirements support narrative, explanation, essay and correspondence work without becoming a separate “connectors unit.”

## X04 — Social-pragmatic repertoire, politeness and register

**Source-review rows:** 59, 64, 68, 84, 92, 102, 112, 118, 132, 150, 167, 170, 172, 235.

## X05 — Turn-taking, discussion maintenance and active-listening behaviour

**Source-review rows:** 79, 104, 139, 143, 148, 157, 178, 180.  
Keep conversation moving, invite others in, signal ending, interrupt politely, and show active listening while supporting or disagreeing. These are participation conditions inside C06/C07/C11/C15, not independent topic content.

## X06 — Contextual inference and lexical meaning deduction

**Source-review rows:** 1, 10, 12, 19, 23, 70, 71.  
Use headings/context and familiar subject knowledge to predict/infer, identify useful subject vocabulary, and derive likely meaning of unknown language. Evidence must remain context-bound; this is not unrestricted guessing or dictionary-free mastery.

## X07 — Written self-editing accuracy

**Source-review rows:** 229.  
Check and correct spelling, punctuation and grammar in simple written text. This is a quality-control requirement across C17–C20 rather than a standalone proofreading capability.

# Exit-contract overlays carried into later stages

These are not additional source rows and are not included in the 249-row accounting. They come from the B1 exit contract and constrain curriculum mapping, lifecycle design and later semantic reconciliation:

1. **Independent familiar-situation operation:** B1 must move beyond A2 routine exchange into less predictable but still familiar life/travel/work/personal-interest situations.
2. **Connected linear discourse:** speaking/writing evidence must support connected narrative and straightforward connected text, not only sentence chains.
3. **Reasoned response:** the learner must give brief reasons for opinions, plans and actions in more than one context.
4. **Unprepared interaction:** at least some oral interaction must be genuinely unprepared on familiar topics.
5. **Repair/circumlocution remains legitimate:** hesitation and lexical limits remain compatible with B1 if meaning stays clear and the learner can repair/work around gaps.
6. **Pronunciation remains intelligibility-first:** noticeable first-language influence and regular mispronunciation may remain; later pronunciation review must support intelligibility rather than accent imitation.
7. **A2 is prerequisite, not new inventory:** locked A2 routine transactions, short linked descriptions and bounded repair are retrieved and extended, not silently reclassified as new B1 targets.

# Stage-01 critic decisions

1. **Do not copy A2’s 26-capability / 12-unit / 83-lesson shape.** B1 ability evidence independently yields **20 capability families + 7 cross-cutting requirements** at Stage 1; no unit or lesson count is set here.
2. **Keep practical information/instructions (C01) distinct from general factual/media comprehension (C02).** “Find/follow the needed information” and “understand the main points/details” have different success conditions.
3. **Keep correspondence reception (C03) visible.** Familiar letters/emails carry event/feeling/wish information and later feed C17 production, but reading correspondence is not replaced by writing one.
4. **Keep narrative reception (C04) distinct from narrative production (C09/C18).** Shared story content does not erase modality or independence boundaries.
5. **Keep viewpoint/argument reception (C05) distinct from opinion/reason production (C07).** Recognising a writer’s line/viewpoint is not proof that the learner can formulate and justify their own.
6. **Treat unprepared familiar conversation (C06) as a genuine B1 progression beyond A2 routine exchange.** It must not be reduced to scripted small talk.
7. **Keep repair/circumlocution (C08) as a reusable performance capability.** B1 explicitly requires paraphrase, glossing, rephrasing and lexical-gap management beyond simple repetition requests.
8. **Keep source transformation/mediation (C16) distinct from generic comprehension and opinion.** Summarising/reporting/collating information changes the learner task and must remain separately visible.
9. **Keep presentation/Q&A (C11) distinct from generic familiar-topic speaking (C10).** Audience delivery and follow-up questions create a different evidence boundary.
10. **Keep travel/directions (C12), service/product problems (C13), and health (C14) separate.** They are all practical, but their outcomes and safety/precision boundaries differ.
11. **Do not create fake capabilities for politeness formulas, turn-taking, cohesion, inference or proofreading.** X01–X07 preserve these as mandatory conditions/resources inside real performances.
12. **Do not treat one integrated source statement as multiple inventory records.** No SPLIT is required at Stage 1; if later evidence exposes genuinely separable performance obligations, reopen explicitly without inflating the source count.
13. **No Stage-01 row is silently excluded.** All 249 rows have exactly one primary capability/cross-cutting home; later stages may add cross-links without changing source accounting.
