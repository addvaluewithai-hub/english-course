# Architecture change from Stage 03

## Add X08 — Reference-building, noun-phrase expansion and substitution

Grammar rows **59–65, 86–94, 202–213, 261–264, 273–301** (with supporting links from G39) make reference tracking and information packaging too important to leave implicit.

**X08 requirement:** learners must increasingly build, identify and track reference across connected B1 discourse using bounded relative clauses, expanded noun phrases, possessive/reflexive reference, demonstrative back-reference, `one/ones` substitution, indefinite/quantity pronouns and related prepositional structures.

**Why cross-cutting, not a capability:** reference-building has no independent real-world endpoint. It improves C02/C03/C10/C16/C17/C18/C19 and reduces repetition while keeping information clear. It must be practised inside those performances.

Architecture after Stage 03: **20 top-level capabilities + 8 cross-cutting requirements**.

# Stage-03 dependency / control flags for later stages

These are review flags, not final semantic-role decisions:

1. **Conditionals need role-aware separation.** G13 real/future conditions and G14 imagined/counterfactual conditions are canonical B1, but later semantic/lifecycle review must not assume identical productive depth for every row, especially the past-counterfactual/regret branch.
2. **Reported speech is functionally owned by C16/C17, not by a grammar chapter.** Stage 4.5 must verify mediation/relay and correspondence evidence paths before lesson mapping.
3. **Passive voice remains embedded.** G39 supports factual/process/media comprehension and production, but no source performance requires a standalone passive capability.
4. **Past systems must be sequenced by narrative purpose.** Past continuous/perfect/simple and `used to` support background, prior-time, sequence and past-vs-present comparison; do not build a tense-table unit.
5. **Present perfect simple/continuous extends A2, not resets it.** B1 must retrieve locked A2 experience use and add duration/recent relevance/continuing activity without re-teaching the whole system as new.
6. **Modals are function-led.** Ability/permission/expectation, possibility, obligation/deduction, advice, regret, politeness/indirectness and prediction cannot be assessed interchangeably because they share forms.
7. **Reference infrastructure is now explicit as X08.** Stage 04 words and Stage 05 mapping must use it to support denser connected discourse, not create a noun-phrase unit.

# Stage-03 critic decisions

1. **No new top-level capability is required.** All 338 canonical rows naturally serve the 20 performance families plus cross-cutting infrastructure.
2. **Add X08, but do not create a reference/relative-clause capability.** The large reference system is a means of producing clearer connected discourse, not a real-world endpoint.
3. **C07 remains function-led despite heavy conditional/modal evidence.** Advice, solutions, alternatives, opinions and reasons share grammar resources, but B1 is not being reorganised as a modal/conditional syllabus.
4. **C09 is strongly validated as connected narrative.** Coordination, past continuous, past perfect, expanded past simple, `used to`, future-in-past and narrative-related modal uses provide a real language base distinct from generic familiar-topic description.
5. **C16 is strongly validated as source transformation/mediation.** Reported speech, reporting clauses, comparison/discourse relations and reference tracking support summarising/reporting/collating rather than mere comprehension.
6. **C11 presentation remains ability-led, not grammar-led.** Discourse markers, focus and question/interaction resources support it, but no special “presentation grammar” capability is needed.
7. **C03/C17 correspondence boundaries survive.** Grammar gives both richer reference, reporting, politeness and connected-text resources; reception and production/interaction must still be assessed separately.
8. **C12 travel and C13 service/problem handling remain distinct.** Shared requests/modals/prepositions do not erase different real-world outcomes.
9. **X02/X03 are again confirmed as central B1 infrastructure.** Canonical linking/subordination/discourse-marker evidence matches Stage-02’s 41 discourse-related phrase homes.
10. **Phrase-form flags were checked rather than promoted blindly.** `as if`, `be supposed to`, `even though`, `If I were you`, `ought to` and several other flags have canonical B1 grammar support; any phrase without broader support remains bounded to its reviewed chunk/function.
11. **338 EGP rows are not 338 teaching objects.** The 59 systems are also not automatically 59 lessons; several are embedded infrastructure or multiple rows describing one usable performance resource.
12. **A2 remains prerequisite/retrieval.** Shared forms such as present perfect experience, future arrangements, advice/request modals and simple reporting must be treated as cumulative foundations where already locked, with only genuinely new B1 functions/control introduced as B1 inventory.
