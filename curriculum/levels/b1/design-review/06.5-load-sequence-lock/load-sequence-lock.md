## Final structural decision

The corrected Stage-05 skeleton remains **12 units / 85 lesson slots**.

The original 84-slot map was reopened during Stage 06 because C18 review/familiar-topic essay writing did not have a sufficiently explicit independent home. `U10-L03 — Write a short review or familiar-topic essay` was added, producing the current 85-slot architecture.

After that correction, the exact 1,581-record ledger and human load/sequence critic found no further reason to add, remove, merge or split a lesson before semantic-role reconciliation.

The **85 count is now a load-and-sequence decision**, not merely a Stage-05 baseline.

## What the critic tested

The review challenged the 85 slots for:

- one coherent primary communicative/evidence outcome;
- preservation of modality and interaction boundaries;
- branch fit inside broad capabilities such as familiar-topic description/explanation;
- accidental vocabulary/topic dumping;
- accidental grammar dumping caused by raw EGP row counts;
- prerequisite systems appearing before dependent performances;
- A2 carry-forward appearing as retrieval rather than relabelled B1 content;
- late introductions that would lose practice/fresh retrieval;
- new-content leakage into transfer/final-integration slots;
- fresh retrieval in changed contexts rather than model repetition;
- explicit paths for the B1 exit products: unscripted familiar problem, connected narrative and reasoned response in more than one context.

Raw source-row count is treated as a **mechanical warning**, not as a teaching-object count.
