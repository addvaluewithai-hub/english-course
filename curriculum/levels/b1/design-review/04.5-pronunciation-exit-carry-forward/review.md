# A. B1 pronunciation review — 10 / 10 strands

Global boundary: **B1 pronunciation is intelligibility-first.** The learner may remain noticeably accented and may mispronounce regularly. The curriculum must improve recognition, clarity, prominence, chunking and self-repair sufficiently for connected familiar-topic communication; it must not target accent imitation or accent elimination.

## P01 — Sound perception and word recognition
**Source:** `pronunciation.sound_perception.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C02 factual media/listening, C04 narrative reception, C06 familiar conversation, C15 interviews.  
**Progression:** recycle A2 sound perception → extend to familiar connected speech with more natural variation.  
**Evidence:** identify familiar words/main points in new connected input without requiring perfect phonetic discrimination.

## P02 — Consonant clarity
**Source:** `pronunciation.consonant_clarity.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C06/C07/C09/C11/C12/C15/C17.  
**Progression:** consolidate contrasts that materially affect intelligibility across longer exchanges and connected output.  
**Boundary:** regular errors may remain if the intended message remains generally intelligible.

## P03 — Vowel clarity and reduced vowels
**Source:** `pronunciation.vowel_clarity.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C06/C09/C11/C12/C17 plus C02 listening.  
**Progression:** consolidate enough vowel distinction and reduced-vowel perception for familiar connected speech.  
**Boundary:** accent difference is not itself failure.

## P04 — Syllable shape, consonant clusters and word endings
**Source:** `pronunciation.syllable_shape.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C09 narrative, C11 presentation, C13 practical problem explanation, C17 correspondence-to-speech/online interaction.  
**Progression:** extend A2 word-shape control into longer stretches and self-correct important cluster/ending breakdowns.

## P05 — Audible grammatical endings
**Source:** `pronunciation.grammatical_endings.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C09/C10/C15/C16/C18, especially narrative, reporting, comparison and connected factual description.  
**Progression:** make time, number and ownership contrasts sufficiently audible in connected speech.  
**Boundary:** pronunciation evidence serves grammatical meaning; it is not an isolated suffix drill target.

## P06 — Word stress
**Source:** `pronunciation.word_stress.b1`  
**Natural homes:** distributed through C01–C20 where new B1 lexis is actively used; stronger evidence in C11 presentation and C15 interview.  
**Progression:** generally intelligible stress across longer familiar-topic output plus repair of important errors.

## P07 — Sentence prominence and rhythm
**Source:** `pronunciation.prominence_rhythm.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C07 reasoned response, C11 presentation, C15 interview, C16 reporting/mediation, C06 conversation.  
**Progression:** move beyond sentence-by-sentence delivery; use prominence to make main information/reason/contrast clear.  
**Cross-link:** X02 main-point hierarchy.

## P08 — Connected-speech perception and production
**Source:** `pronunciation.connected_speech.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C02/C04 reception; C06/C07/C09/C11/C15 interaction/production.  
**Progression:** process familiar connected speech and use linking/reduction only where it supports rather than obscures meaning.

## P09 — Intonation, interaction and stance
**Source:** `pronunciation.intonation_stance.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C06 social conversation, C07 opinions/advice, C08 repair/checking, C11 Q&A, C15 interview; X04/X05.  
**Progression:** signal main intent, involvement, contrast, completion and checking in familiar interaction.  
**Boundary:** pragmatic intelligibility matters more than copying a native-speaker contour.

## P10 — Chunking, pausing, monitoring and pronunciation repair
**Source:** `pronunciation.chunking_monitoring_repair.b1`  
**Natural homes:** C06/C07/C08/C09/C11/C15/C16 and the B1 unscripted-problem evidence path.  
**Progression:** keep connected familiar-topic speech moving, pause at meaningful boundaries and repair pronunciation without abandoning the message.  
**Cross-link:** B1 fluency permits visible planning pauses; successful repair is part of control, not evidence of failure.

### Pronunciation critic

- All **10 / 10** strands have natural capability homes.
- No standalone pronunciation unit is required by the evidence.
- Longer connected output, presentation, interview, narrative and unprepared interaction create natural fresh-retrieval points.
- Pronunciation must be distributed and later lifecycle-audited like other requirements.
- Accent reduction/perfection is explicitly out of scope.

# B. B1 exit-contract constraints

The B1 exit promise is cumulative through A1+A2+B1 and cannot be satisfied by source-row exposure alone.

## E01 — Independent operation in familiar situations
**Exit claim:** operate independently in familiar life, travel, work and personal-interest situations using connected language.  
**Architecture consequence:** practical performance cannot remain A2-scripted/routine only. C06, C07, C12, C13, C15 and C17 need changed-detail/fresh tasks with reduced support.

## E02 — Main points of clear standard input and familiar media
**Exit claim:** understand main points of clear standard input and relatively clear media.  
**Architecture consequence:** C02/C04/C05 require repeated listening/reading across content domains; one media lesson cannot prove the exit claim.

## E03 — Connected narrative
**Assessment requirement:** **a connected narrative**.  
**Architecture consequence:** C09/C18 need a multi-lesson path from event sequence → background/prior time → reactions/outcomes → fresh connected narrative. C04 provides reception models but may not substitute for production evidence.

## E04 — Reasoned response in more than one context
**Assessment requirement:** **a reasoned response in more than one context**.  
**Architecture consequence:** C07 cannot be evidenced once. Opinion/reason/choice/advice/solution language must recur in at least two meaningfully different contexts and later fresh retrieval.

## E05 — Unscripted familiar problem
**Assessment requirement:** **an unscripted familiar problem**.  
**Architecture consequence:** C07/C08/C12/C13/C06 require at least one fresh problem with incomplete information or a changed condition, forcing clarification/circumlocution and a practical outcome without a complete model.

## E06 — Unprepared familiar conversation
**Exit claim:** enter unprepared conversation on familiar topics.  
**Architecture consequence:** C06 must progress beyond A2 small-talk routines into topic continuation, turn-taking and spontaneous response with planning pauses allowed.

## E07 — Straightforward connected writing
**Exit claim:** produce straightforward connected text on familiar/personally interesting topics.  
**Architecture consequence:** C17/C18/C19 need genre-distinct writing evidence plus X03 paragraphing/cohesion and X07 self-editing. Email alone cannot prove all writing claims.

## E08 — Written/online interaction
**Exit claim:** interact about experiences/feelings, seek clarification and answer comments in some detail.  
**Architecture consequence:** C17 must include reply/response chains rather than only one-way messages.

## E09 — Mediation / source transformation
**Exit claim:** convey familiar information from clear texts, seek simple compromise and support shared communication.  
**Architecture consequence:** C16 must include real source-to-output transformation (summarise/report/compare/collate/relay), not merely comprehension questions. Stage-03 reported-speech G53 is therefore confirmed as **C16/C17 infrastructure**, not a new grammar capability.

## E10 — Range with hesitation and circumlocution
**Exit claim:** enough familiar-topic language for some unpredictable situations, with hesitation/circumlocution when limits appear.  
**Architecture consequence:** C08 circumlocution/repair must be embedded across the level and appear in fresh evidence; unknown-word workaround is legitimate B1 control.

## E11 — Reasonable grammar accuracy, not error-free control
**Exit claim:** intended meaning remains clear despite errors/L1 influence.  
**Architecture consequence:** Stage-03 systems need role-appropriate control, but authoring must not convert B1 into error-free form mastery. Complex/counterfactual/passive/reporting rows may later be productive, formulaic, receptive or support according to semantic evidence.

## E12 — Connected linear discourse
**Exit claim:** link shorter elements into a connected linear sequence with simple paragraphing and chronology.  
**Architecture consequence:** X02/X03/X08 are exit-critical infrastructure across C07/C09/C10/C11/C16/C17/C18/C19. They cannot be postponed to a final writing unit.

## E13 — Fluency with planning/repair
**Exit claim:** keep going comprehensibly despite evident pauses for planning and repair.  
**Architecture consequence:** transfer/evidence tasks must reduce model dependence while allowing normal B1 hesitation; fluency is sustained meaning, not speed.

# C. Locked A2 carry-forward map

A2 is a locked prerequisite. Its semantic/design state is not edited here. B1 retrieves and extends A2 obligations only where B1 performance depends on them.

## CF01 — A2 routine social contact → B1 unprepared familiar conversation
**A2 basis:** C01 social contact/small talk + C20 repair; short direct familiar exchange.  
**B1 extension:** C06 sustained/unprepared familiar conversation + X05 turn-taking + C08 paraphrase/circumlocution.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** refresh early through changed social prompts; do not reteach greetings/wellbeing as new B1 inventory.

## CF02 — A2 background/profile exchange → B1 interviews, work/education and connected personal explanation
**A2 basis:** C02 personal/family/education/work background + C24 simple survey/interview.  
**B1 extension:** C10 connected explanation, C15 interview with follow-up/checking, C17 applications/correspondence.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** assume core profile/question repertoire retained; retrieve it inside richer interviews/applications.

## CF03 — A2 preferences/opinions/comparison/advice → B1 reasoned choice and problem solving
**A2 basis:** C06 opinion/reason, C07 comparison, C13 advice/suggestions, C12 requests/permission.  
**B1 extension:** C07 compare options, justify viewpoints, advise, suggest and solve familiar problems with broader condition/modal/discourse resources.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** A2 simple reason/comparison/modal routines are prerequisites; B1 introduces greater independence, alternative evaluation and reasoned response across contexts.

## CF04 — A2 factual past + simple narrative → B1 connected narrative
**A2 basis:** C16 past events/experience and C17 simple narrative sequence.  
**B1 extension:** C09/C18 connected narrative with background, prior time, past-vs-present comparison, reactions/outcomes and wider cohesion.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** retrieve simple past, bounded present-perfect experience and A2 sequence language before B1 past-perfect/expanded narrative perspective.

## CF05 — A2 plans/arrangements/predictions → B1 future perspective and planning explanation
**A2 basis:** C14 invitations/arrangements and C15 plans/intentions/predictions.  
**B1 extension:** C10/C12/C17 use wider intention/prediction, future continuous, future-in-past and changed-plan/reporting language where functionally needed.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** do not reteach A2 `be going to`/arrangement basics as new; retrieve before new B1 functions.

## CF06 — A2 directions/travel/reservations → B1 less-routine travel problems and changes
**A2 basis:** C10 directions + C11 tickets/tourist info/reservations.  
**B1 extension:** C12 detailed directions, transport needs, ticket/reservation changes and less-routine travel situations.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** route/ticket/reservation basics are prerequisite; B1 evidence adds changed conditions, problem handling and less predictable interaction.

## CF07 — A2 shopping/services → B1 complaints and product/equipment problem resolution
**A2 basis:** C09 routine transaction + C18 instructions/process.  
**B1 extension:** C13 explain need/problem, negotiate price/delivery, understand solution advice, request action and complain.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** routine purchase/request/quantity language is retrieval; B1 teaches the problem-resolution layer.

## CF08 — A2 practical information/main point → B1 broader factual media and argument reception
**A2 basis:** C22 targeted practical retrieval + C23 short clear factual/descriptive main point.  
**B1 extension:** C01 practical search/instructions, C02 longer familiar factual/media input, C05 viewpoint/argument/attitude.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** scanning/main-point basics are reused early and repeatedly; B1 adds denser input, logical relationships and viewpoint recognition.

## CF09 — A2 short messages/correspondence → B1 formal/detailed correspondence and online interaction
**A2 basis:** C21 short everyday correspondence/messages + invitation/arrangement writing.  
**B1 extension:** C17 personal/formal email/letter, application/enquiry, advice, response chains and online comments.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** basic opening/closing and simple message clarity are assumed/retrieved; B1 adds detail, purpose, register, response and connected structure.

## CF10 — A2 simple instructions/process → B1 practical informational/instructional texts
**A2 basis:** C18 simple instructions/rules/process.  
**B1 extension:** C19 rules, notices, leaflets, listings, device/care instructions plus C13 problem handling.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** imperative/order basics are prerequisite; B1 adds longer explanation, condition, passive/reference and audience clarity.

## CF11 — A2 basic health → B1 richer but still bounded health communication
**A2 basis:** C19 simple medical form/advice/symptom talk.  
**B1 extension:** C14 medication/instruction reading, symptoms with somewhat more precision and health-information forms.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** retain survival boundary; no specialist diagnosis/prescription expansion.

## CF12 — A2 rehearsed public delivery → B1 presentation + Q&A
**A2 basis:** X08 short rehearsed public statement/announcement and Unit-12 presentation evidence.  
**B1 extension:** C11 short familiar-topic presentation with clear structure and simple factual follow-up questions.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** reading/rehearsed delivery is prerequisite only; B1 requires message organisation, prominence and handling questions.

## CF13 — A2 relay/mediation → B1 source summarising/reporting/mediation
**A2 basis:** convey main points of short simple everyday texts; routine message relay; supported collaboration.  
**B1 extension:** C16 compare/collate/summarise/report clear sources and relay useful information; seek simple compromise.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** simple factual relay is retrieved; B1 source transformation must involve selection, organisation and reporting rather than copying.

## CF14 — A2 linked simple output → B1 connected linear discourse
**A2 basis:** short series linked by frequent connectors; simple sequence/contrast/examples.  
**B1 extension:** X02/X03/X08 + C09/C10/C11/C16/C17/C18/C19 connected linear discourse, paragraphing, reference tracking and chronology.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** A2 `and/but/because`-level cohesion is assumed; B1 builds a wider but still controlled discourse repertoire.

## CF15 — A2 pronunciation intelligibility → B1 longer connected intelligibility
**A2 basis:** generally understandable familiar-situation speech with possible repetition/listener effort.  
**B1 extension:** generally intelligible connected familiar-topic speech despite regular mispronunciation/L1 influence, with prominence, chunking, connected-speech processing and self-repair.  
**Carry-forward treatment:** recycle all lower-level sound/stress/intelligibility work inside new B1 performances; do not reset pronunciation as isolated sounds.

# D. Whole-architecture Stage-4.5 critic

## D1 — Modalities remain distinct
Reception capabilities C01–C05 are not replaced by production tasks merely because B1 expects greater independence. C04 narrative reception and C09 narrative production remain distinct; C03 correspondence reception and C17 correspondence production/interaction remain distinct.

## D2 — C07 is not a grammar bucket
Conditionals/modals/comparison/discourse resources serve opinion/advice/solution performance. Shared forms do not permit merging permission, prediction, deduction, regret and advice into one “modals lesson.”

## D3 — C10 remains a capability family, not a unit
The broad lexical/content range in C10 is intentionally distributed across the course. Unit structure must be built around concrete performances and dependencies, not “familiar topics.”

## D4 — C16 mediation is confirmed
Ability evidence, lexical source-handling evidence, B1 exit mediation and Stage-03 reported-speech systems jointly justify C16. No new reported-speech capability is needed.

## D5 — C08 repair is an exit-critical reusable capability
B1 circumlocution, clarification, confirmation and rephrasing must occur in other capabilities and in unscripted evidence. It cannot be taught once and forgotten.

## D6 — Pronunciation has natural homes
All ten strands can be embedded in real B1 performances. No isolated pronunciation unit is justified.

## D7 — A2 is prerequisite, not duplicated B1 inventory
Routine transaction, simple linked description, bounded repair, A2 past/future/comparison/modal basics and short messages are retrieved as foundations. Only B1-new functions/control are introduced as B1 content.

## D8 — Exit evidence requires spacing
The three explicit B1 assessment requirements—unscripted familiar problem, connected narrative, reasoned response in more than one context—must each have introduction/practice/fresh retrieval and later evidence. None may be hidden only in a final unit.

## D9 — No architecture reopening required before Stage 5
After pronunciation, exit and carry-forward review, the current **20 capabilities + 8 cross-cutting requirements** remain coherent. No source evidence justifies adding/removing a top-level capability at this point.
