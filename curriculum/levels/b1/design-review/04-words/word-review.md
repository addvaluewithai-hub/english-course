This file records the human curriculum decisions after reading all **809 B1 Oxford lexical records**. It does not duplicate the canonical inventory and does not decide final productive/receptive mastery before curriculum-map and lesson evidence exist.

The exact-home policy is frozen to the reviewed `words.json` Git blob. Explicit semantic sets receive one primary home; the remaining records intentionally receive `LX-GENERAL`, meaning they were reviewed but do not have a stronger single semantic teaching home. Any source-blob change invalidates the expansion and requires renewed human review.

## Executive decision

- **No new top-level capability is required by the B1 lexical inventory.**
- Stage-03 architecture remains **20 capabilities + 8 cross-cutting requirements**.
- The words strongly broaden the **contexts** in which B1 performances occur: work/education, media, public issues, environment, health, practical problems, travel, services, technology, science, culture and leisure.
- This breadth must **not** become a sequence of topic-vocabulary units. Content attaches to C01–C20 performances.
- The Stage-02 warning around **C10** is resolved architecturally: C10 is not a giant “talk about topics” unit. Its lexical breadth is distributed across multiple content sets and reused in reception, narration, opinion, mediation, practical interaction and writing.
- A B1 word-list member is not automatically an independent productive exit target. Final productive/receptive/support/grammar-owned status belongs to Stage 8.
- Grammar-heavy words/function items remain controlled by Stage-03 systems and cross-cutting discourse/reference requirements.
- Duplicate surface strings and new senses remain source-record-specific; headword matching is permitted only where the frozen inventory has a unique exact surface row.

# Architecture impact after lexical review

1. **No new capability added.** Environment, politics/public life, science, technology, culture, sport and business are content domains rather than independent communicative endpoints.
2. **C10 survives, but not as one unit.** Lexical breadth is intentionally distributed through L01–L27 and must attach to real description/explanation, narrative, opinion, mediation, practical and writing performances.
3. **C02/C05/C16 become important homes for broad factual content.** This prevents forcing every public/science/environment/culture word into independent speaking production.
4. **C13 practical problem handling is well supported** by service, equipment, repair and consumer language without requiring specialist technical vocabulary.
5. **C14 remains narrow and practical.** Health/body lexicon supports symptom/instruction/form work while preserving the non-diagnostic boundary.
6. **C16 mediation/reporting is lexically strengthened** by source/report/summary/reference/statistic/translation language, but meta-language itself need not all become productive.
7. **X02/X03/X08 remain central.** Evaluation, discourse-relation and reference vocabulary must improve connected B1 discourse rather than become contentless vocabulary lessons.
8. **Same-word/new-sense discipline remains mandatory.** Exact source-record identity outranks surface spelling; ambiguous duplicate headwords must never be guessed by automation.

# Stage-04 critic

- **Rejected:** creating units called “Politics”, “Environment”, “Science”, “Technology”, “Culture”, “Business” or “Sport” solely because lexical sets exist.
- **Rejected:** treating all 809 records as active vocabulary. The semantic lock will decide productive/receptive/support/grammar-owned control after lesson evidence exists.
- **Rejected:** assigning a word to multiple primary homes just because it can appear in several contexts. Primary ownership stays exactly one; cross-links remain pedagogical reuse.
- **Rejected:** alphabetic/range-based semantic classification. The generated word aid was read as lexical evidence; home decisions are semantic and the remainder is explicitly reviewed/frozen.
- **Kept:** `LX-GENERAL` as a transparent critic queue rather than inventing weak topic homes for every word.
- **Kept:** broad reception/mediation performances as natural homes for content breadth that learners should understand but may not need to independently produce.
