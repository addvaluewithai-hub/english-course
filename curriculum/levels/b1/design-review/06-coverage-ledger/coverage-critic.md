## Human load decision

The mechanical warning thresholds remain unchanged. They are diagnostic thresholds over raw source rows, not claims about independent teaching objects.

After the first schedule exposed extreme clustering, Stage 06 was manually rebalanced inside human-approved lesson windows. The worst raw totals fell substantially, while exact coverage, lifecycle completeness and zero-new transfer protection were preserved.

The remaining warnings are recorded exactly in `load-critic.json` and are **accepted provisionally for architecture purposes**, for these reasons:

1. Multiple EGP rows frequently belong to one reviewed grammar system and therefore do not imply one separately taught rule per source row.
2. Broad B1 lexical inventory is often contextual or receptive support. Stage 06 cannot honestly count every lexical row as an active productive teaching object before semantic-role lock.
3. Reception-led lessons can contain wider contextual language than a productive lesson without requiring learner production of every source item.
4. The highest remaining warning, **U10-L02**, is explicitly conditional: Stage 08 semantic roles must constrain active load, and Stage 08.5 must reopen/split it if role-aware teaching-object load is no longer coherent.
5. No warning is accepted as evidence of mastery, productive control, or permission to turn support/receptive material into new lesson targets.
