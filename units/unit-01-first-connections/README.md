# Unit 1 — First Connections

Arabic title: **التعارف والمعلومات الشخصية**

This is the first complete controlled content unit built from the approved A1 lesson
map and the accepted gold lesson. It is a production-ready content package, not a
production database or a batch of rendered media files.

## Unit promise

By the end of the unit, the learner can understand, give, ask for, record, and write a
small set of essential personal details in realistic first-contact situations.

## Boundaries

- Eight approved lesson candidates belong to the unit.
- `Introduce Yourself` remains the reference lesson and becomes `U1-L01`.
- Social politeness and repair are embedded as support; they are not silently claimed
  as separately mastered lessons.
- The identity-mediation candidate is deferred because its required listening
  prerequisite belongs outside this unit boundary.
- Watching video or finishing activities never proves unit mastery.

## Files

- `unit-manifest.json`: outcome, sequence, source-linked candidate ids, and status.
- `unit-mastery.json`: the fresh multi-part unit mission and passing rule.
- `unit-mastery-experience.json`: authored inputs, answers, and replacements.
- `unit-review.json`: delayed review and weak-area routing.
- `unit-accessibility.json`: media, interaction, construct, and privacy requirements.
- `lessons/`: complete packages for lessons 2–8; lesson 1 remains the gold reference.
