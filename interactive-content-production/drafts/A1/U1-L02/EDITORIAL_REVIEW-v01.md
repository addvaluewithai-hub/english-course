## Critical P0 / P1 checks before any paid audio

1. **P0: source owner ruling on 13 disputes.** Do not change Stage-06 lifecycle, or pretend that mere occurrence in audio counts as teaching.
2. **P1: true recorded-message requirement.** The scripted phone sample is not a sound file; real audio must be produced and heard separately from the two-person age conversation. No fake waveform completion or HTML-hidden keyed digits. `zero`/`three` are basic digit prerequisites in the fictional message; check learners can recognize them or add proportionate support. This subtask is *receptive only* here, not the later U1-L05 contact repair task.
3. **P1: potential A1 cognitive overload.** Source dialogue is only 41 words but the lesson currently has **20 Otti beats and six MCs** before genuine Speaking. Time the real audio and Otti speech; try with a novice and remove unnecessary repetitions rather than assuming "short recording = short lesson".
4. **P1: word stress is genuinely audible.** ThirTEEN vs THIRty and nineTEEN vs NINEty require carefully articulated *real audio*, not a fixed/rising-pitch caricature. Quiz controls must wait for teacher PCM or recorded audio to drain.
5. **P1: receptive versus productive evidence.** A learner saying "I'm twenty-four" may achieve age communication, but has not necessarily independently produced the source-locked `X years old`. Distinguish conversation success from exact phrase proof, allow natural retrieval follow-up and record actual learner words.
6. **P2: scope of question grammar.** The two EGP source forms are wider than the two proposed contextual examples. `Are you nineteen?` and `Who is nineteen?` are **bounded**; seek pedagogical/source review before claiming complete interrogative be mastery.
