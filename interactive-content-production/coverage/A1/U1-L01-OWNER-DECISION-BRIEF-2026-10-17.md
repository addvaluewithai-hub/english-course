## Independent counterexample review — separate from the 12 owner gates

### P1 — First-listen multiple-choice **answer leakage** (producer fix requested)

- In `src/lessonScenes/a1/listeningTeacherScript.ts`, the **pre-audio** `first-listen / scene-set` says (in Egyptian Arabic) *Maya and Leo meet for the first time*; the concurrently authored board also states **"Two people meet for the first time."**
- In `src/lessonScenes/a1/listeningTeacherPilot.ts`, the ensuing broad MC asks what the people were doing; the keyed answer is **"They meet, say their names, and ask how they are."**
- **Counterexample:** a learner can correctly choose "meeting" from setup/board without hearing a single phoneme. Therefore MC correctness **does not** independently demonstrate first-listen comprehension even once audio becomes available.
- **Request:** retain a non-answer-bearing prelisten setup (e.g. "You'll hear two people; listen for their purpose") with neutral visual; after actual full listening, use a question answerable from an unheard detail (such as which **two kinds** of information are exchanged), balanced distractors, replay and specific wrong-answer feedback. Test with audio muted and compare whether the question remains trivially answerable. The final lesson must still treat missing WAV as failure, not emulate an audio pass.

### P1 — Required productive phrases **do not have individual evidence**

- The source lock requires **five formulaic productive phrases**: `Good morning`, `How are you?`, `pretty good`, `really good`, `Thank you`. They exist in the authored audio/teacher speech, and the final `fresh-meeting / new-person` task is promising as a communicative scene.
- But the prompt explicitly frames `Pretty good`, `Really good` and `Thank you` as **optional**. A successful learner can naturally choose just *one* wellbeing expression; requiring both in the same conversational reply is unnatural. Thus a plan to offer Speaking **is not evidence for five distinct productive roles**.
- **Request:** author an evidence matrix for each required phrase: (a) where presented, (b) a **genuine independent spoken elicitation** or changed scenario/role, (c) valid alternatives without punishing natural speech, (d) how optional output is reported as `not evidenced` rather than falsely mastered. Use two different wellbeing turns/scenarios if distinct `pretty good` and `really good` need evidence. Preserve one natural final first meeting and verify actual Gemini Live teacher repair with silence/`Okay`/`Bye` loops. The producer and curriculum owner should determine whether phrase-level evidence is due in this lesson or in a documented retrieval lesson, without silent lowering of the lock.
